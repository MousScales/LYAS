What Awaits You

A Day Built Around Comfort, Connection, and Care

The LYLAS Adult Day Program is designed to feel like a second home — not a clinical setting. Guests arrive to a welcoming morning meal, then move through thoughtfully planned enrichment, creative activities, and time outdoors in our fenced courtyard. Every part of the day is guided by caregivers who know each person by name.

Families can expect a calm rhythm: active mornings, a chef-prepared lunch, gentle afternoon programming, and a peaceful wind-down before pick-up. Whether someone needs daily support, respite relief, or occasional visits, the experience stays personal, dignified, and engaging.

Our new space brings together relaxation rooms, a theatre for movies, exercise and arts classrooms, a commercial kitchen led by Director of Culinary Ms. Terra Land, and safe outdoor areas — plus transportation when arranged. It is care with heart, structure, and room to flourish.

Flexible Plans, Clear Pricing

Daily care, respite care, or support days follow the plan of care in place when a client attends. Fees are $35.00 per hour. Families choose how often per week, hours per day, and which days work best.

Continental breakfast, a nutritional lunch, drinks, and snacks are included in the hourly rate.
